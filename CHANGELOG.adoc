=== [Unreleased]

==== Added

* `ZenodoDeposits.jl_chora.deed`: the repo deed at the root. It follows the deed
  grammar and passes `deed_lint.py`, and holds identity, clade, lineage, meta,
  ecosystem, agentic, neurosym, playbook, state and anchor facts.
* CI job `no-a2ml` in `dogfood-gate.yml`: fails when any `.a2ml` file is tracked.

==== Removed

* Every `.a2ml` file (97), `.githooks/validate-a2ml.sh`, and the a2ml
  pre-commit hook. Their facts moved into the repo deed.
* `.machine_readable/arrival-pack/` and `.machine_readable/coaptation/`. `CLAUDE.md`
  is now hand-authored. The `claude-md`, `coapt*` and `state-touch` recipes are gone.
* The a2ml-paired contractiles and three unused composite actions
  (`manifest-check`, `contractile-validation-check`, `semantic-audit-check`).
